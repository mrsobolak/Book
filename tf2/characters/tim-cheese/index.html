Class: The Cheeseman · Tank / Support

He is cheese. He is Tim. He is your best friend. Aged eighteen months in a cave in Wisconsin, he has a Swiss wedge for a head, two googly eyes that never agree on a direction, and a bandolier of wax-dipped cheese wheels he shares with anyone below half health.

How he was built

  • The body is built from metaballs: a barrel chest, gut, traps, huge forearms and big fists, blended into soft TF2-style volumes. Each outfit piece (shirt, skin, gloves, pants, boots) is its own blended mesh.
  • The head is a Swiss-cheese wedge with 24 spherical holes cut by exact booleans and heavy rounded bevels. The rind is shaded darker.
  • Cycles bakes ambient occlusion into the vertices, so the holes and armpits darken.

TF2 look

  • Half-Lambert light through a warm ramp. Shadows turn orange on cheese and red on skin.
  • Sky rim light from above, with Phong specular tuned per material.
  • Ink lines come from an inverted hull at a constant pixel width.

Googly physics

Each pupil is its own node with a damped spring simulation. It feels gravity and the head's acceleration, and bounces off the rim. Spin him, jump him or shake his head and watch them rattle.
