Flow

  • Spawn is behind the intel room; the resupply cabinet faces the door so defenders re-arm on the way out.
  • Three ways across: the plank bridge (fast, exposed to both sniper decks), the dry canal under it (slow, safe from snipers, blind corners), and the north catwalk deck (height, one ramp).
  • The conveyor yard on the north side and the mid shed on the south side break sightlines so a carrier can dodge.

TF2 × CoD

  • TF2: saturated team buildings with a dark stripe, cheese silos, water tower, plank catwalks, oversized signs, a cartoon intel briefcase.
  • CoD: sandbag nests in the courtyards, olive military crates, a stranded troop truck by each base, barbed wire on the perimeter wall, floodlight towers.

Budget

  • Everything is boxes, revolves and a few booleans; one draw call per material; ambient occlusion baked into vertices so no shadow maps are needed.
  • Under 70k triangles for the whole map. The Xbox One pushed twenty times that per frame in 2013.
