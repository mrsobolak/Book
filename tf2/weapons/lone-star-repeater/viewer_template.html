Reference analysis

The clip is a showcase of a community semi-auto shotgun for the Engineer: an item card with blue upsides, red downsides and a one-line joke, then first-person gameplay on 2Fort-style maps.

  • In first person the gun fills the lower right corner. The silhouette has to read at a glance.
  • Surfaces are soft, bevelled slabs, with no sharp CAD edges.
  • Materials stick to a small set: dark blue-grey metal, warm wood, brass accents.

Shape language

  • One family of forms: every part is a side profile extruded and bevelled, or a lathe turned around the bore. Nothing borrows a shape from another gun.
  • Humpback receiver gives it a clear semi-auto look, with a chunky pistol grip and a fat forend.
  • The lone-star medallion on the left side carries the team colour.
  • Proportions are pushed: thicker barrel, bigger trigger guard, oversized brass cap.

Shading recipe

  • Half-Lambert diffuse through a warm light ramp, as TF2 does. Shadows stay readable and never go black.
  • Sky-coloured rim light from above, with Phong specular tuned per material.
  • Wood grain and metal wear come from procedural noise in object space.
  • Ink lines come from an inverted hull that holds a constant pixel width.
