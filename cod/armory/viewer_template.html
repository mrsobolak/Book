Zero references

No images were used. Each weapon is written as numbers in a Python script: profiles, lathes, rods and boolean cuts, sized from how the real mechanisms work. There are 10 scripts on one shared toolkit, with no kitbashing between guns.

Pipeline

  • Angle-limited bevels with hardened normals for machined edges.
  • Cycles ambient occlusion plus convexity and concavity masks, baked per vertex.
  • Meshopt-compressed GLBs. Each gun is decoded only when you open it.

Materials

  • Cerakote, polymer, phosphate and stainless steel, lacquered walnut, brass, satin-ground blade steel.
  • Paint chips on edges and grime collects in recesses. Camo is procedural and applied across every part.
  • Iridescent lens coatings, glowing reticles and tritium sights.
