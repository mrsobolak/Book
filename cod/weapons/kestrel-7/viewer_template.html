Zero references

No images went into this. Every dimension was typed as numbers, working from how a modern 5.56 carbine is put together: MIL-STD-1913 rail at 10.01 mm pitch, 7.4 mm M-LOK slots, a 14-inch barrel, a curved 30-round magazine.

Hard-surface pipeline

  • Each part is a profile prism or a lathe. Pockets, ports, slots and sockets are exact boolean cuts.
  • Angle-limited bevels (0.4–2.5 mm) with hardened normals catch highlights the way machined edges do.
  • Cycles bakes ambient occlusion into the vertices. Convexity and concavity are stored next to it.

Materials

  • Physically based shading, lit by an HDR studio environment with ACES tone mapping and soft shadows.
  • Cerakote chips back to bare aluminium on convex edges. Grime collects in recesses.
  • Stippled grip, cast polymer, phosphate steel, an anti-reflection-coated lens and an illuminated reticle.
